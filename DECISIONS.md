# DECISIONS

Decisions made while building the GLOOD site without check-ins, with the reason for each.
Newest thinking is at the bottom of each section.

## Files and folders

- **Source files moved into `./source-assets`.** The brief expected `./source-assets` and `./glood-logo.png`,
  but the folder had `Assets/`, `STICKER/`, `Stickers/` and two loose images. I moved them (nothing deleted):
  - `Assets/Glood_Logo.png` → `source-assets/Glood_Logo.png`, and copied to `./glood-logo.png`
  - `STICKER/STICKER/*` → `source-assets/sticker-board/` (153 PNGs, the zip was already extracted and identical)
  - `STICKER/STICKER.zip` → `source-assets/STICKER.zip`
  - `Stickers/*` → `source-assets/sticker-set/` (10 transparent PNGs + a print PDF)
  - the ChatGPT size chart → `source-assets/size-reference.png`, the board photo → `source-assets/board-photo.png`
- **Raw source images are not committed to git.** They total about 360 MB (GitHub rejects big pushes and
  it would make every clone slow). `.gitignore` excludes `source-assets/`. Only the optimised WebPs (about
  3.3 MB for all 153 designs), the original SVGs and `glood-logo.png` are committed. Your originals stay on
  your disk untouched.
- **The GitHub repo is created as private.** It still deploys on Vercel for free. Make it public in GitHub
  settings if you want.

## Copyright screening

- Verdicts are in `scripts/process_assets.py` (one line per file) and the generated `ASSETS.md`.
- Rule used: anything showing a character, logo, livery, crest, album art, show branding, a real person's
  likeness, or another seller's watermark is risky. Plain text phrases with no logo or show styling are safe.
- Borderline calls, erring on the cautious side: "NINE-NINE!" (show colours) risky, "SUIT UP!" (plain type) safe,
  "Idiot" with a platform-style verified badge risky, the Jesus "I saw that" painting risky (copyright status
  unclear), "BOX BOX BOX" safe (no team or tyre-brand logo).
- 5 board files are low-res copies of `sticker-set` files, so the higher-res versions are used.
- The two print PDFs and the board photo are reference only.

## Logo

- The SVG is **traced from the PNG**, not redrawn, so it matches exactly: each colour layer is split into a mask
  (`scripts/logo_masks.py`) and vectorised with potrace (`scripts/trace-logo.mjs`). Letter fills are traced
  slightly grown so they tuck under the black outline with no hairline gaps.
- Side-by-side check (`scripts/compare-logo.mjs`, output `screenshots/logo-compare.png`): 99.49% of pixels
  match, mean colour difference 0.64/255. The remaining differences are anti-aliased edges.
- The SVG viewBox is the artwork plus an orange margin (the PNG crop at source px 68,1010 to 3276,3048), not
  the whole tall PNG canvas, so it sizes sensibly on the page.
- Eyes are separate groups (`.glood-eye-left`, `.glood-eye-right`), each clipped to the inside of its O so the
  pupil can move and blink without leaving the letter. Brows and smile are separate paths too.
- Colours from the PNG: `--orange #ffa905`, `--red #e10b19` (the D), `--cyan #00bbd1`, `--black #000000`,
  `--white #ffffff`. The G is a slightly pinker red, kept as `--crimson #e10b37`.
- A die-cut sticker version (`src/assets/glood-logo-sticker.svg`) is generated from the same trace for the laptop lid.

## Original stickers

- 12 designs, generated by `scripts/build-stickers.mjs` so they share one style. Text is converted to outlines
  so the files render the same as `<img>` and as WebGL textures (web fonts do not load inside SVG images).
- The chibi character is **Mochi-chan**, a GLOOD original: cat-ear hoodie in brand cyan, orange fringe.
- Meme stickers: BRB Overthinking, Skill Issue, Main Character Energy, Chai > Everything, Touch Grass? Later.
  Extra designs beyond the brief to give every filter at least two originals: Protein > People (Gym),
  Pit Stop For Snacks (Racing), Ramen Mode On (Anime vibe).

## Site

- Vite + plain JavaScript, Three.js, GSAP + ScrollTrigger. Fonts are self-hosted from `@fontsource`
  (the same free Google Fonts, no external requests): Lilita One headings, Bungee labels, Space Grotesk body.
- Laptop is built from `RoundedBoxGeometry` (base, lid, keys, trackpad, screen). Lighting is Three's built-in
  `RoomEnvironment` (no HDR files to download) plus a key and rim light. The floor shadow is a soft baked
  blob that follows the laptop, which is far cheaper than real-time shadow maps and looks like a studio render.
- Stickers on the lid are thin planes parented to the lid, so they turn and open with it. I chose planes over
  `DecalGeometry` because the lid top is flat (a decal would give the same result) and planes can be scaled and
  tilted for the slap animation without regenerating geometry every frame.
- The logo "becomes" the first sticker by flying the DOM logo to the projected screen position of its lid slot,
  then cross-fading into the 3D sticker as it slaps down.
- The lid opens toward the camera, so the sticker-bombed back of the lid turns away and the screen, showing a
  sticker gallery preview, lights up. The real gallery follows directly below.
- Mobile (under 768px wide): 9 stickers instead of 15, lower pixel ratio, smaller textures, no antialiasing.
- Motion off (nav switch): no pinned scroll story, no eye tracking, no CSS animation. The finished
  sticker-bombed laptop is rendered once as a still. See "Owner changes" below for why this is a switch.
- No WebGL: the story section falls back to a flat sticker collage.
- Peel hover is pure CSS: the corner is clipped off the sticker and a mirrored, paper-coloured copy of that corner
  is folded back over it. It works on any die-cut shape, not just rectangles.
- Brand story copy avoids invented facts (no founding year, no customer numbers). Please rewrite it in your own
  words. The ₹20 / ₹30 / ₹50 size bands come from your `size-reference.png`; each sticker's own price comes from
  VentureAdda (see round 3 below).
- The Instagram link is a placeholder in `src/config.js`.

## Changes made after looking at the screenshots

- **Stickers on the 3D lid are unlit** (`MeshBasicMaterial`, no tone mapping). Lit materials washed the print
  colours out under the key light. Instead, each frame dims them slightly by how far the lid faces away from
  the key light, so they still sit in the scene.
- **The flying logo is centred with `inset: 0; margin: auto`, not a CSS `translate`.** GSAP folds an existing
  CSS `translate` into its own transform and dropped the vertical half, so the logo landed 260px too low.
- **The laptop starts just below the frame on every screen size.** A fixed "off-screen" height was visible on
  phones, where the camera sits further back, so the hero pose now uses an `enter` amount scaled to the
  visible height.
- **The open laptop pulls the camera back on portrait screens** (`fit` in the pose), because the 3/4 view of an
  open laptop is much wider than the closed lid.
- **Stickers are kept inside the lid edges**, using each sticker's rotated size, so none hang off into the air.
- **The peel starts at each sticker's real corner.** Peeling the bounding-box corner showed nothing on round or
  irregular stickers, so the build scripts measure how far the artwork sits in from that corner
  (`peelInset` in the manifests) and the fold starts there.
- **Peel flaps are clipped with `overflow: hidden`.** Their mirrored images sit outside the card before clipping,
  and on phones that widened the page to 462px.
- **Build warning:** the only warning left was Vite's 500 kB chunk notice for Three.js (550 kB, 138 kB gzipped).
  Three.js is already split into its own chunk and loaded after first paint with `import()`, so I raised the
  limit to 600 kB in `vite.config.js` with a comment, rather than hide a real problem.

## How it was tested

- `npm run build`: no errors, no warnings.
- `npm run shots`: Playwright at 1440px and 390px, captures the hero, logo landing, mid-slap, fully
  sticker-bombed, lid open, gallery, peel hover, Fresh Drops, story, footer, product page, cart and checkout,
  plus the motion-off still. The script fails if the page logs any console error, page error or failed
  request. The final run was clean.
- `npm run test:shop`: 41 end-to-end checks of the shop (categories, fixed sizes and VentureAdda prices, add to cart, cart steppers, bad cart
  data dropped, checkout validation, the exact WhatsApp message, cart cleared after ordering, Buy now leaving
  the cart alone, remembered buyer details, unknown sticker page, motion switch on and off). All pass.
- Extra checks: the page is exactly 390px wide on a phone (no sideways scroll); the pupils move about 30 units
  toward the pointer on each side; a blink was caught mid-close; scrolling to the end and back gives a
  pixel-identical mid-slap frame (0.00% difference) and restores the hero.
- Not measured: real frame rate. Headless Chromium renders WebGL on the CPU, so its numbers mean nothing. The
  scene only re-renders when the scroll timeline moves, uses about 30 draw calls, and caps pixel ratio at 2
  (1.5 on phones).

## Owner changes (round 2, 2026-09-24)

- **All designs are on the site, including the 120 flagged for copyright or trademark.** Owner's call after
  seeing the screening. `USE_FLAGGED = True` in `scripts/process_assets.py` controls it; set it to `False` and
  run `npm run assets` to take them down again. `ASSETS.md` still records every reason. Risk stays as written
  there: licensed characters, team and club marks, artists' art and brand logos can get listings removed or
  bring legal notices.
- **Four more filters** so 165 stickers stay browsable: Sports, Music, TV & film, Superheroes. Car designs sit
  under Racing.
- **Motion is always on at load, with an on/off switch in the nav.** This overrides the original brief's
  "respect prefers-reduced-motion" at the owner's request, because many Windows machines ship with animations
  off and would never see the scroll story. The switch is on every page and remembered on the device.
- **Shop pages, modelled on VentureAdda.** `sticker.html?s=<slug>` for each sticker (size, quantity, Add to cart,
  Buy now, similar stickers) and `cart.html`. Like VentureAdda, the cart lives in localStorage (no account, no
  server) and is validated on every read. Only slug, size and quantity are stored; prices come from the size
  chart every time, so a stored cart can never show an old price.
- **Checkout is VentureAdda's flow without Firebase.** Name, WhatsApp number, address, "ordering for someone
  else", cash on delivery or UPI. Placing the order opens WhatsApp with the order written out (sticker, size,
  quantity, total, order id, payment, buyer) and the buyer presses send. No payment is taken on the site, which
  keeps the "no paid services, no API keys" rule. VentureAdda also writes orders to Firestore; GLOOD has no
  backend, so the WhatsApp message is the order.
- **GLOOD's WhatsApp number is a placeholder** (`WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `src/config.js`). Until it is set, WhatsApp
  opens with the order and asks the buyer which chat to send it to, and the checkout says so.
- **Buy now orders just that sticker** and leaves the cart as it is, which is what people expect from Buy now.

## Owner changes (round 3, 2026-09-24): one fixed size per sticker

- **No size picker.** Every sticker is printed at one size and its price is its size band (₹20 Small, ₹30 Medium,
  ₹50 Large). Product pages show that size; cards show size and price; cart and WhatsApp lines carry the size.
- **Prices are copied from GLOOD's VentureAdda listing** (`scripts/import_prices.py`, `npm run prices`). All 155
  VentureAdda products are priced; menu items there are public by design, so no key or login is needed. Each
  product is matched to its sticker by picture, one to one, and every pair was checked by eye
  (`.logo-work/va-matches.jpg`), including look-alikes such as Hello Single and Hello Taken. The three stickers on
  the size chart agree with VentureAdda (F1 Logo ₹20, Nine Nine ₹30, Kaali Sadi Aurat ₹50).
- **VentureAdda product names are the titles**, so both shops call each sticker the same thing.
- **Two VentureAdda products had no file here** (Aviation Warning Meme, Single AF). They were added from their
  VentureAdda photos. Those photos are 260 px, so these two are softer than the rest; swap in the originals if
  you have them.
- **Before VentureAdda was pointed out, I tried measuring sizes from the board photo.** It was rejected and the
  script deleted: the board lays stickers out in equal grid cells (a 2 in and a 3.5 in sticker look the same
  size there), and matches at that resolution were often wrong.
- **The 12 GLOOD originals are not on VentureAdda**, so they show as Medium (₹30) until they are listed there
  or given a size in `sizes.json`.
- **Old carts** saved while sizes were selectable are merged into one line per sticker, not thrown away.

## Deployment (2026-09-24)

- **Live at https://glood.vercel.app**, on the owner's Vercel account, project `glood`, connected to the GitHub
  repo so every push to `main` redeploys. Vercel builds from source (`npm run build`); `.vercelignore` keeps the
  360 MB of raw source images, screenshots and local work files out of the upload.
- Checked after deploy: every page and asset returns 200 to a logged-out visitor, hashed assets are cached for a
  year, and every `test:shop` check passes against the live URL (`GLOOD_URL=https://glood.vercel.app/`).

## Owner changes (round 4, 2026-09-24): faster story, categories people can actually see

- **The scroll story takes about half the scrolling**: 3.5 screen heights on desktop (was 6.5), 3 on phones
  (was 5), and it catches up with the scrollbar in 0.5 s (was 0.8 s). Timeline and frames are unchanged.
- **Categories are now hard to miss.** The owner liked the nine categories but found the filter chips too easy
  to overlook, so:
  - a "Shop by category" section of big coloured tiles, each showing three of its stickers and a count, sits
    straight after the scroll story (the first thing a visitor reaches);
  - a Categories menu in the nav on every page, labelled on phones too (the text nav links are hidden there);
  - the filter chips stay pinned under the nav while browsing the grid, as one row (swipeable on phones);
  - category links are shareable (`index.html?cat=racing#gallery`), and a product page's category tag and a
    "See all ..." button lead to its category. On the home page the menu filters in place instead of reloading
    the 3D story.

## Owner changes (round 5, 2026-09-24): sticker backdrop and cursor

- **Stickers sit on a dark "laptop lid" backdrop** everywhere they appear (gallery cards, Fresh Drops, product
  page, cart and checkout thumbnails). On the old white and cream panels, the stickers' white die-cut borders
  merged into the background. The dark lid matches the 3D laptop and makes every border read. Shadows were
  deepened so the hover lift still shows on dark.
- **Cursor follower** (`src/js/cursor.js`): a yellow, black-outlined ring trails the pointer, grows over
  anything clickable, says View / Peel / Open over cards, the product sticker and category tiles, and throws a
  small burst of sticker confetti on click. The normal cursor stays visible (no loss of precision or
  accessibility). Mouse only; hidden on touch screens, while a checkout dialog is open, and when motion is off.
  The animation loop stops as soon as the ring catches up, so an idle page does no work.
