    How to play

    The rule

    Your bike never stops and leaves a solid line of light behind it. Touch any line and you crash: a rival's, your own, or the arena wall. Last rider still riding wins the round.

    Tricks

    Boost to cut in front of rivals. Ride close beside a line without touching it to grind: your boost refills faster. When a rider crashes, their line dissolves a moment later (you can switch this off in the room settings).

    Jump to hop over a line. You can't jump over buildings or the arena edge, you crash if you land on a line, and you can jump again after a couple of seconds.

    Heights

    Some arenas have raised decks, trenches and tunnels. Ramps take you up and down; ride off an edge to drop to the level below. Lines on different levels never touch, so you can pass over or under them. You can't steer in the air or on a ramp. Kickers throw you high over lines, and gold boost pads give a burst of speed. On Afterglow lines fade after 4 seconds.

    Pick a vehicle on the start screen: they all ride the same, so it's about style. Turn on Change arena every round in the match settings for a new arena each round.
