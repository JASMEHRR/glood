202603 Situation & market

Rules are stricter. Habits aren't.

Solid Waste Mgmt Rules, 2026

In force 1 April 2026. Households must sort into 4 streams: wet, dry, sanitary, special care.

E-Waste (Mgmt) Rules, 2022

In force 1 April 2023. Producers' recycling targets rise from 60% to 80% by 2027-28.

Today's habits

Sold to scrap dealers, mixed with general waste, or stored at home until a chance comes.

SWOT

W Ops and partners to build

O People want simpler sorting

T Reliability, prices, density
