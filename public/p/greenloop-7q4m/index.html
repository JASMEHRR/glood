?!02 The problem

Every flat has a pile nobody collects.

It piles up

Old phones, cables, clothes and plastic sit in storerooms for months.

It goes to landfill

Mixed waste ends up at Dadumajra. Very little is recovered.

Zero reward

Segregating feels like a chore with no visible result.

RWAs can't prove it

Societies must comply but have no tracking or reports.
