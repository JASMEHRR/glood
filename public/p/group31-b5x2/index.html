Phase D · Controls, formulas & limits

54 functions in about 142,500 formulas, zero errors, behind locked structure

Navigation & protection

  • MasterSheet links to A1 of all 15 tabs
  • "◀ Back to MasterSheet" in A1 of every other tab
  • Workbook structure locked
  • Only search, input and scenario cells unlocked
  • Dashboard and model unprotected so slicers filter

Formula inventory

  • Text & date: TRIM, CLEAN, PROPER, VALUE, DATEVALUE, DATEDIF
  • Lookup: XLOOKUP, INDEX + MATCH, VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, CHOOSE
  • Logic: IF, AND, OR, IFERROR, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, SUBTOTAL
  • Finance & stats: PMT, PV, STDEV.S, SKEW, CORREL, LINEST
  • Tools: Data Tables, Goal Seek, slicers, validation

Assumptions & limits

  • PD 2% / 5% / 12% by rating; LGD 45%
  • Cost of funds 6.0%; opex 1.5%; 5-year EMI term
  • History covers only 33% of loans
  • May 2025 is partial (data ends 5 May)
  • XLOOKUP needs Excel 365 / 2021+; re-run Goal Seek after input changes
